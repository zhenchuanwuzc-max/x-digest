Hector Serrano： 第一个原因，说起来可能有点老套，但我觉得最重要的是，Wispr 能以这样的速度增长，归根结底是因为产品。从一开始，增长就是由产品驱动的。

I joined at the pre-product stage, and I remember we had a few thousand users early on, and just out of nowhere just started catching steam. And six months in, I remember Tanay said it, he was like, "Wow, we really found PMS. It found like we struck gold."

我在产品还没成型时就加入了。记得最初只有几千个用户，后来突然就开始起势。大约六个月时，我记得 Tanay 说：“哇，我们真的找到 PMS 了，感觉像挖到了金矿。”〔译注：原贴为 PMS，按上下文疑指 PMF，即产品与市场契合；英文不改，音频未核。〕

And then from there, just adding on all these other channels, whether it be organic, paid, everything is just adding more fuel to the fire.

从那以后，无论再加自然流量、付费流量，还是其他渠道，都相当于给已经燃起来的火再添燃料。

And what we found a lot of success with through trial and error, doing a bunch of different things, me testing a bunch of channels, other people testing a bunch of channels, is one of the main ones we've landed on now is paid and we're funneling that with a lot of UGC content.

我们通过大量试错取得了不少成果。我试了很多渠道，其他人也试了很多。现在确定下来的一个主要渠道就是付费投放，并用大量 UGC 内容来支撑它。

And we have a lot of pillars inside of paid, whether that be UGC video, statics, influencers, in-house videos, founder storytelling.

付费投放内部又有好几根支柱，包括 UGC 视频、静态素材、网红合作、内部制作的视频和创始人故事。

总体而言，不管是 Meta、LinkedIn、YouTube 还是其他平台的付费广告，确实已经成为 Wispr 的主要支柱之一，而我负责其中的内容工作。

2.1 用冷流量测试表达

Hector Serrano: Well, I think paid is actually one of the best channels to invest on early on. And I learned this from Matt, who is actually our ex-head of growth at Wispr. He's now at Viktor.

Hector Serrano： 其实我觉得，付费是最值得早期投入的渠道之一。这是我从 Matt 身上学到的。他以前是 Wispr 的增长负责人，现在在 Viktor。

And a lot of his lessons and lessons that I've learned from him is taking this e-com approach to SaaS, which a lot of companies are scared to turn on the paid engine. And what happens when you actually turn on the paid engine is you actually learn a lot faster if the messaging actually resonates with your audience.

我从他那里学到了很多，其中一个重点，是把电商的做法带到 SaaS。很多公司不敢启动付费引擎。但真正开始付费投放后，你会更快知道：你的传播信息到底能不能打动目标受众。

Are you actually able to convert this cold traffic into users? And at the end of the day, most companies aren't that skilled in organic content because it is something very new.

你能不能把这些原本不认识你的冷流量转成用户？说到底，大多数公司并不擅长自然内容，因为这是一个很新的领域。

And unless you have really young people on your team who really understand these organic channels really well, know how to package it, know how to make the content, it's really hard to actually get any clear numbers on does your product actually have legs? Do people actually resonate with it?

除非你的团队里有非常年轻、真正懂这些自然流量渠道、懂包装、懂制作内容的人，否则你很难获得清晰的数据，来判断产品到底有没有发展潜力、用户究竟有没有共鸣。

Are you actually able to track these conversions, et cetera? So I think paid makes sense from the get-go. Obviously testing within your limits and your budgets that you have.

你能不能追踪这些转化，等等？所以我觉得从一开始做付费是有道理的。当然，要在自己的能力和预算范围内测试。

But yeah, if you're able to see I'm able to turn this cold traffic into paying users, then you have a clear signal there that this same messaging or this product will also work on organic if you optimize things a bit more.

如果你发现，自己能够把冷流量转成付费用户，那就是一个清楚的信号：再优化一下，同样的传播信息或同样的产品，也可能在自然流量渠道奏效。

Lenny Rachitsky: I want to come back to that of just what that early team may look like, even maybe pre-product market fit. But what's really interesting about what you're sharing here is paid has been around for a long time, running ads on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, all these things. There's nothing new as a platform.

Lenny Rachitsky： 我还想回头聊聊这样的早期团队应该是什么样，甚至在找到 PMF 之前。但你说的有一点很有意思：付费投放已经存在很久了，在 Instagram、Facebook、TikTok 上跑广告，平台本身没什么新鲜的。

And what you're saying here is there's still a lot of alpha and opportunity to grow substantially and do something different in paid.

而你的意思是，付费渠道里仍然有很多尚未被充分利用的优势和机会，可以带来显著增长，也可以做出不同的东西。

Hector Serrano: Yeah, 100%. And a lot of times people also get scared because they'll say, "Oh, I don't want to turn on the paid engine yet or spend a bunch of money because then we're going to reach a point where we oversaturate this market or this ICP."

Hector Serrano： 对，百分之百。人们也经常害怕，说：“我现在还不想启动付费引擎，也不想花太多钱，否则我们会很快把这个市场或这一类理想客户群打透，达到饱和。”

But at the end of day, it's just getting smarter with how you're, again, positioning the product, and there's always new messaging, new ICPs you can target, and you won't really reach saturation if you're savvy enough with the angles that you're approaching your content with, or how you're approaching that paid mechanism, I guess.

但说到底，你只需要更聪明地思考产品定位。总会有新的传播信息、新的理想客户群可以触达。如果你足够善于选择内容切入角度，或者设计付费增长机制，我觉得其实不会那么容易饱和。
